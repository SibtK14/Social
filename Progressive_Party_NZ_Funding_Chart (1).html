Who We Are

A party built for every community in Aotearoa

The Progressive Party believes that every New Zealander — regardless of background, postcode, or income — deserves access to great schools, reliable healthcare, and a government that listens. We are here to make real, lasting change.

  • Closing the gap between wealthy and disadvantaged communities

Our Platform

What we will do when you give us your trust

📚

Education

  • Introduce a single, consistent national assessment framework
  • Raise educational standards from early childhood through secondary school
  • Invest in teacher training and increase teacher numbers
  • Transition private schools to a low-fee public model — with fee waivers for low-income families
🚌

Transport

  • Significantly expand public transport infrastructure across NZ
  • Invest in reliable, affordable services that reduce private vehicle dependence
  • Better-connected communities mean happier, more productive New Zealanders
  • Improved transport access as a driver of economic participation
🎸

Sport & Culture

  • Require all under-16s to participate in organised sport
  • Create publicly funded instrument hire schemes for schools and community groups
  • Develop dedicated event infrastructure — hosted events contribute back to NZ
  • Support the arts through access and participation, not just grants

Our Health Plan

A healthier Aotearoa, with care that works

Healthcare is one of our main priorities. We will strengthen the public system by growing its workforce, making everyday food staples more affordable, and investing in research and better-run hospitals.

🩺

Grow the healthcare workforce

Increase the number of jobs in New Zealand's healthcare system and guarantee a public-system role for doctors and nurses who graduate from medical school in New Zealand. A larger workforce will help reduce waiting times.

🥦

Make healthy food more affordable

Subsidise basic foods such as bread, vegetables, meat and other staple goods to support healthier choices and help tackle obesity.

🔬

Invest in research and better hospitals

Direct research funding towards cancer prevention and finding better ways to run hospitals efficiently.

Responsible Finance

Funding a better future, carefully

Our spending commitments are real, so our funding plan has to be too. We will raise revenue from those best placed to contribute — while protecting the vast majority of New Zealand households.

Better public transport reduces household spending; a stronger healthcare workforce reduces long-term system costs. Progress pays for itself.
